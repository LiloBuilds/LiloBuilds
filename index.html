Meet Lilo
Quality control. Napping on the job since 2021.

My dog, and the inspiration behind it all. She's been napping on the job since 2021, occasionally stepping in as quality control and general morale support.

The hands and skills behind the work are mine. I'm Oz, an Amsterdam-based craftsman with over 10 years of hands-on experience in carpentry, repairs, building work and furniture.

I started this company to make building and maintenance feel more straightforward and approachable. Clear communication, transparent costs, easy planning, and honest advice.

Fetch. Fix. Finished. I'll sort it, start to finish, and leave you with something done properly.

And if you're after simple, honest work and don't mind helping keep Lilo well fed, I'm your guy.
